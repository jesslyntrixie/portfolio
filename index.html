Publications

  1. Cross-Output Saliency Comparison for Multi-Output Image Regression in Nutritional Estimation

    ICORIS 2026 (IEEE) · First author · Accepted, to appear in IEEE Xplore

    Compared four explanation methods (Grad-CAM, SHAP, LIME, Chefer) on ResNet-50 and ViT-B/16 models that predict several nutrition values from one food photo. LIME separated the outputs most; Chefer barely changed between them.

    Paper link coming soon

  2. Enhancing Decision Tree Performance through Stacking Ensemble Learning for Sentiment Analysis

    Procedia Computer Science (ICCSCI 2025) · Scopus Q2 · Second author

    Decision trees are easy to interpret but weak at sentiment analysis on their own. On IMDB movie reviews, stacking a decision tree with an SVM raised accuracy from 73.46% to 88.08%.
